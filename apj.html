About APJ Hostel

APJ Abdul Kalam Hostel is one of the most modern and well-equipped hostels at Delhi Technological University. Named after India's beloved former President and renowned scientist Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam, this hostel embodies the spirit of innovation and excellence in student accommodation.

Established in 2018, the hostel features contemporary architecture with a focus on providing a comfortable and conducive environment for academic pursuits. The building is designed with energy-efficient systems and modern amenities to ensure the best living experience for our residents.

Hostel Philosophy

Following Dr. Kalam's vision of nurturing young minds, APJ Hostel creates an environment that promotes learning, innovation, and personal growth. We believe in providing not just accommodation, but a home away from home where students can thrive academically and personally.

Hostel Rules & Guidelines

General Rules

• All residents must maintain a valid student ID and hostel registration

• Entry and exit timings: Gates close at 11:00 PM and open at 5:00 AM

• Visitors are allowed only in designated areas during specified hours (9:00 AM - 8:00 PM)

• Consumption of alcohol and tobacco products is strictly prohibited

• Ragging in any form is a serious offense and will lead to immediate expulsion

Room Guidelines

• Keep rooms clean and well-maintained at all times

• No unauthorized electrical appliances allowed

• Room changing is permitted only with prior approval from hostel administration

• Damage to hostel property will result in penalty charges

Academic Focus

• Maintain noise levels conducive to studying, especially during examination periods

• Regular attendance in classes is mandatory

• Academic progress will be monitored and parents will be informed of any concerns
